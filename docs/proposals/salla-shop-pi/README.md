3. Personalize and send an **Outreach Draft** (only with confirmed/accurate claims).

## Scope & claims (important)
- **Sandbox-first**, narrow pilot: **Price + Payment Integrity** (e.g. 100 EGP quoted in Pi) before broader expansion.
- CoreGuard is an **independent verification/evidence layer**; it does **not** replace Salla’s payment, wallet, escrow, or dispute-decision systems.
- Outcomes are **named and reason-coded** (`VERIFIED`, `MISMATCH`, `EXPIRED`, `REPLAY_DETECTED`, `PENDING`, `UNKNOWN`) — not fraud guarantees.
- No partnership, endorsement, funding, user-count, or ecosystem-status claims are implied.
