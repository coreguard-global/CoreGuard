## Why 87 failed reads are not 87 defects

Every negative construction in the set is built to fail a check, so a reader that reads it correctly reports `verification_failed` — 87 of the 143 reads are exactly that, and only 6 are the layer declining to judge at all. The expectation column beside it agrees on 143 of 143 vectors with 0 false accepts. The observation column records what the reader found; the interop column records whether that finding was the one the corpus built for. Neither column can be read as the other.

## The five outcomes on this set

| Outcome | Reads here | What it means when it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | 50 | the bytes this reader recomputed matched the claim the record makes — not that the record is valid, and not that the work happened |

| `not_checked` | 1 | the reader never claimed this layer, and the reason says which of the six it is |
| `absent` | 2 | the record carries no anchor profile at all; a Nostr-shaped member is a different record, not a failed anchor |

The six `not_checked` reasons are frozen, and one appears on this set: `outside_scope` (1 row). A reason is not decoration — `access_refused` is re-scope the caller, `unsupported_family` and `schema_not_implemented` are a build limitation named as such. The other five are exercised with real refusals on [AER1-OBSERVATION-LEDGER.md](./AER1-OBSERVATION-LEDGER.md).

## What the join shows, and what it refuses to show

- The `aer1-kit:per-vector` observer column is present for **66 compared rows** and unclaimed elsewhere. On those 66 rows the divergence count is **0**; on family-level-only kit lines there is no per-vector verdict to compare, so the page does not invent one.
- The joined verdict column has no divergence against the reference kit on 66 compared vectors. The observation columns add reads; they do not move that number.
- These rows carry no timestamps. A clock value on a corpus read would be invented, and the layer refuses invented time where it matters: the ledger page exercises the append-order rule with a real backfilled read and a named refusal (`OUT_OF_ORDER`).
- Reading 143 vectors is not 143 runs. The observation layer never turns a read into an execution, and this page states `executions` nowhere because these are fixtures, not runs.
- A row here can say `verified` while the corpus calls the same vector invalid. That is the layer working: `verified` is a property of the bytes a reader recomputed, never of the record's validity.

## Boundary

- No conformance claim, and no new vocabulary: every outcome on this page is one of the five the corpus already uses, and every reason is one of the six the ledger froze.
- The observation family is additive by construction. It cannot change a verdict, a false accept or a divergence; it records who read what, when that matters, and why a check did not happen.
- Nothing here observes the world. A matching digest settles what the saved record says, never whether the purchase, delivery or transfer it describes occurred.
