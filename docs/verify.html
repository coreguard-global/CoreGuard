What a PASS means: the signature verifies against the pinned issuer key, the digest recomputes from the receipt's own fields, this envelope hashes to the fingerprint the receipt names, the network agrees, and the clock is inside the authorization window.
What it never means: that the transaction is safe, that the counterparty is honest, or that anything executed. A pre-sign receipt carries executionProof: NOT_INCLUDED; execution is proven by the ledger read-back, separately.
