3. Bump version in the same commit:
   - `packages/agent-commerce/package.json` → `0.1.3`

## 5) Honest commercial split (MIT OSS vs paid services)

| Layer | Offering | Model | Boundary |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Open Source (MIT) | `@coreguard/agent-commerce` MCP/SDK/local verification primitives | Free | No hosted SLA, no managed multi-tenant control plane, no custody/payment processing claims |
| Pro (future) | Policy management, reporting, integrations, team workflows | Subscription | Only for capabilities actually built and supported |
| Enterprise (future) | Central policy control, audit/reporting, SSO/RBAC, SLA, private integrations | Contract | Requires separate hardened service architecture; do not imply available today |
| Integration/Verification services | Pilot integration, verification service, support | Service/usage/contract | Scoped by SOW; no claims beyond delivered/tested scope |

## 6) What not to claim

- No claim that the MCP prevents all fraud or guarantees safety (`VERIFIED ≠ SAFE`).
- No claim of cross-process/distributed replay protection (process-local only until built).
- No claim of live merchant/payment/wallet/PSP integration unless implemented and tested.
- No claim of endorsements/partnerships/certifications/downloads-as-users.
