02 · Core concepts

The CGEP/1 evidence chain.

intent→policy→commitment→receipt→independent verifier→anchor

ALLOW

Execution matched authorization and evidence is complete.

DENY

Policy or evidence failed — fail-closed, nothing moves.

HOLD

UNKNOWN stays held until evidence closes it.

VERIFIED ≠ SAFE — a receipt proves what happened matched what was authorized, not that the action was a good idea.
